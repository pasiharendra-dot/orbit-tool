1. Refund Policy Overview

At GetPerfectResume.com, we provide an instant resume rewriting service. Because our service delivers your rewritten resume immediately upon upload and payment, and we delete all files from our servers right after delivery, we operate a strict no-refund policy.

By using our service, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • The rewritten resume is delivered to you instantly and automatically
  • Once delivered, the service has been fully rendered
  • All files are immediately deleted from our servers after delivery
  • Refunds cannot be issued for completed, delivered services

This policy applies to all purchases made through our website.

2. Exceptions to No-Refund Policy

While we maintain a strict no-refund policy due to the immediate delivery of our service, we may consider refunds only in the following exceptional circumstances:

2.1 Technical Failures

A refund may be issued if:

  • You were charged but did not receive your rewritten resume due to a technical error on our end
  • The service failed to process your upload and you received no deliverable
  • Our system experienced a critical failure that prevented service delivery

Note: You must contact us within 24 hours of the failed transaction with proof of payment to request a refund for technical failures.

2.2 Duplicate or Unauthorized Charges

Refunds will be issued for:

  • Duplicate charges for the same service
  • Unauthorized charges that you did not approve
  • Billing errors clearly attributable to our system

2.3 Service Not Rendered

If you were charged but our service was completely unavailable and you received no rewritten resume, a refund may be issued upon verification.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will NOT be provided in the following cases:

3.1 After Service Delivery

  • You received your rewritten resume successfully
  • You downloaded or accessed the rewritten resume
  • You are dissatisfied with the quality or style of the rewriting
  • You changed your mind after receiving the deliverable
  • You made an error in uploading the wrong file

3.2 Quality Disputes

  • Subjective dissatisfaction with writing style or format choices
  • Preference for different wording or phrasing
  • Disagreement with suggested improvements
  • Expectations not aligned with service description

3.3 User Error

  • Uploading an incorrect or incomplete resume file
  • Providing poor quality or unreadable source material
  • Losing the downloaded file after delivery
  • Technical issues on your device or internet connection

3.4 Change of Mind

  • Deciding you no longer need the service after purchase
  • Finding a different service provider
  • No longer requiring a resume

Important: By using our service, you acknowledge that the resume rewriting and delivery happens instantly and automatically. Once the service is rendered and the file is delivered to you, the transaction is complete and cannot be reversed.

4. How to Request a Refund (Exceptions Only)

If you believe you qualify for one of the exceptional circumstances listed above, please follow these steps:

1

Contact Support Immediately

Email us at support@getperfectresume.com within 24 hours of the transaction

2

Provide Transaction Details

Include your order/transaction ID, payment receipt, account email, and a detailed explanation of the technical failure or billing error

3

Submit Evidence

Provide screenshots, error messages, or other documentation that supports your claim of technical failure or unauthorized charges

4

Wait for Review

Our team will investigate your claim within 2-3 business days and verify the technical issue or billing error

5

Receive Decision

You will be notified via email whether your refund request has been approved or denied, with a detailed explanation

Required Information for Refund Requests:

  • Full name associated with the account
  • Email address used for registration
  • Transaction ID or receipt number
  • Exact date and time of purchase
  • Detailed description of the technical failure or billing error
  • Supporting documentation (screenshots, error messages, etc.)

Important: Refund requests submitted more than 24 hours after the transaction will not be considered unless there are exceptional circumstances beyond your control.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

In the rare event that your refund request is approved:

  • Processing Time: 5-7 business days from approval
  • Credit/Debit Cards: Refunds typically appear within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank
  • PayPal: Refunds typically appear within 3-5 business days
  • Other Payment Methods: Timeline varies by provider, typically 7-14 business days

Note: Approved refunds are processed promptly, but the time it takes for the refund to appear in your account depends on your financial institution or payment provider.

6. Understanding Our Service Model

GetPerfectResume.com operates on a pay-per-use model:

6.1 How It Works

  • You upload your resume and make a one-time payment
  • Our system immediately processes and rewrites your resume
  • You receive an editable copy of the rewritten resume instantly
  • All files are deleted from our servers immediately after delivery

6.2 No Subscriptions

We do not operate on a subscription model. Each resume rewrite is a separate, one-time transaction. You only pay when you need our service.

6.3 Multiple Uses

If you need to rewrite another resume or update your existing one in the future, you would make a new purchase at that time.

8. Chargebacks and Disputes

We strongly encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider.

8.1 Why Contact Us First

  • We can verify whether you received the service and resolve legitimate issues quickly
  • We can provide proof of service delivery if there's a misunderstanding
  • Chargebacks may result in account suspension or termination
  • We may be able to assist with technical issues or questions about your delivery

8.2 Chargeback Consequences

If you initiate a chargeback:

  • Your account will be immediately suspended pending investigation
  • We will provide evidence to your bank that the service was delivered as promised
  • If the chargeback is found to be illegitimate, your account may be permanently terminated
  • You may be held responsible for any fees or costs associated with the chargeback dispute
  • Future access to our services may be denied

8.3 Legitimate Chargebacks

Chargebacks are appropriate for:

  • Fraudulent or unauthorized charges you did not make
  • You were charged but received absolutely no service or deliverable
  • Technical failure where we cannot provide proof of delivery

Service Verification: We maintain logs of all service deliveries. If you claim non-delivery, we can verify whether the rewritten resume was successfully delivered to you before processing any dispute.
