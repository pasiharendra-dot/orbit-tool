    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about our ATS optimization engine.

    What exactly does GetPerfectResume.com do?

    We take your existing resume and instantly restructure it to bypass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). Our recruiter-trained AI rewrites your bullet points for maximum impact and outputs a perfectly structured Microsoft Word document in under 60 seconds.

    How is this different from ChatGPT?

    ChatGPT gives you raw text that you still have to format, and it frequently invents fake experiences. Our AI resume builder is hardcoded with strict recruiter guardrails: zero hallucination, exact ATS keyword structuring, and it instantly generates a fully formatted `.docx` file ready to submit.

    Will the AI make up fake experience?

    Absolutely not. We have a strict "Zero Seniority Hallucination" protocol. We will never elevate your job level or invent achievements. We simply translate your raw career data into the high-impact language that recruiters look for.

    What file format will I receive?

    You will instantly download a perfectly formatted Microsoft Word document (`.docx`). We provide Word files rather than PDFs so that you retain full control to make minor edits or update your contact info in the future.

    Do I need a specific Job Description (JD)?

    You can optimize for general industry standards, but pasting a target JD is highly recommended. It allows our AI resume maker to customize your 12 core skills and summary to match the exact keywords the employer is scanning for.

    Is my data secure and private?

    100% yes. Your uploaded resume is used strictly for the 60-second generation process. The original file is instantly and permanently deleted from our servers the moment your new resume is generated.
