The "Resume Black Hole" isn't a myth. It's an algorithm.

Most job seekers apply to hundreds of roles and hear nothing back. It’s not your experience—it’s your formatting. Modern hiring relies on software like Taleo, Greenhouse, and Workday to filter candidates before humans are involved.

Broken Layouts

Using Canva, heavy graphics, or complex multi-column tables? Parsers scramble this data into a single, unreadable block of text, causing instant rejection.

Missing Hard Keywords

ATS platforms rank you based on precise keyword density from the Job Description. If you say "managed data" but they want "SQL analytics", you lose.

Weak Action Verbs

Saying "Responsible for" or "Helped with" triggers low-performer flags. The AI looks for high-impact vocabulary like "Spearheaded", "Architected", or "Scaled".

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a "Good" ATS Score?

A score above 80% means your resume format is machine-readable and contains the fundamental sections required by most parsers (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse). Above 90% indicates strong keyword alignment, lack of formatting errors, and high density of quantifiable achievements.

Is this ATS Checker really free?

Yes! You can upload your resume, parse your data, and view your baseline ATS score completely free. You can also preview your data in our templates for free. If you want to download the optimized Word Document or use our premium AI re-writer to fix errors, we offer highly affordable upgrades.

Why is my beautiful Canva resume failing?

Graphic design tools often export text as flattened images, nested objects, or use complex floating text boxes. A human can read it perfectly, but a robot parsing the code just sees a blank page or jumbled letters. Our templates solve this: they give you beautiful design layered over clean, 100% parseable code.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We do not sell your personal data. Your uploaded resume is securely parsed in the cloud via enterprise-grade encryption to generate your score, and is kept strictly confidential within your personal workspace.
