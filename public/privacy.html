Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Auto-Deletion (Core Policy): GetPerfectResume.com (operated by Orbit Careers) employs a strict zero-retention policy for your documents. The PDF you upload is temporarily loaded into our server memory exclusively for AI processing. The moment the processing is complete, your original PDF is permanently and automatically deleted from our servers (`fs.unlinkSync` protocol). We do not store, sell, or manually review your uploaded resumes unless you specifically opt into the "Expert Human Review" package.

2. Information We Collect: We collect your Name, Email, and Phone Number (extracted automatically by the AI or provided by you) for the sole purpose of delivering your optimized resume and providing customer support.

3. Payment Processing: All payments are processed securely via Razorpay. We do not store or process your credit card information on our servers.
