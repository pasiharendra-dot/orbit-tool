Our Founder

A Recruiter Who Got Tired of Watching Good Talent Get Filtered Out

GetPerfectResume.com was born from a very specific frustration — seeing brilliant candidates rejected not because of their skills, but because their resume couldn't pass an algorithm they didn't even know existed.

After spending 17 years placing candidates at companies ranging from growing startups to Fortune 500 firms, Harendra Pasi saw the same story play out thousands of times. Talented professionals were being filtered out by ATS software before a single human even looked at their profile.

Rather than coaching candidates one by one, Harendra encoded 17 years of recruiter knowledge into an AI — so every job seeker could access the expertise of a top-tier HR consultant.

"Every time a good resume fails an ATS scan, a career gets delayed. That is the problem we exist to solve."

— Harendra Pasi, Founder, GetPerfectResume.com
